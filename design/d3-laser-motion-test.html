Laser cutting

A focused beam melts steel locally; an assist-gas jet drives the melt out through a narrow kerf as the head follows a planned contour.

Piercing

The cut starts inside the material that will be removed, then a short lead-in joins the contour.

Cutting the holes

The head cuts each bolt hole in turn, moving along the fixed beam between the flanges.

Kerf

Material is removed only along a narrow kerf. The lens is a drawn section, not a measurement.

Finished part

The bolt holes are complete. The end cut is drawn as a trace around the section and the offcut is shown as an exploded view; the head's path around the flanges is not modelled.

Choosing how a part is cut

My PhD develops a BIM-integrated method for choosing cutting technologies and machines in steel fabrication. It reads parts from the design model, estimates cost, time and fabrication-stage carbon for each machine option, searches for project-level plans, and reports how stable a recommendation is.
