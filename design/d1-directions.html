Computational Matter · design stage D1 · for Murat Güven’s review

Three directions, the same content

Each direction is drawn on the identical hero, laser chapter and MSc chapter, so the differences come from art direction alone. The 3D frames are rendered live in your browser with three.js from one generic I-beam model built to the laser reference sheet. Science frames use only recorded optimization states from the MSc thesis.

Illustrative render: generic geometry, no process valuesRecorded states: images taken from the thesis, in their real orderVisual: an arrangement for presentation, not a result

Direction A

Steel / Light

A pale gallery stage with one machined steel object. CAD linework resolves into material across the hero. The laser chapter is a controlled dark bay set inside the light page, then the page returns to light.

Direction B

Digital Fabrication Cinema

Near-black, material-led and directed like a short film. Extreme macro views of nozzle, kerf and steel carry the identity from the first frame. Spectacular, at the risk of reading as a fabrication company.

Direction C

Computational Atlas

Bright and exact. Structural members, toolpaths and recorded optimization states appear as an annotated atlas of linework. Strongest on substance, quieter on emotion.

Hybrid tested as a fourth candidate

A and C tie. The candidate hybrid takes A’s light identity and hero, C’s atlas treatment for the science and AI chapters, and B’s macro camera for the laser chapter only, inside A’s dark bay. Scored on the same criteria:

It beats the best single direction by 2 points, gaining on originality and motion and losing one point of feasibility. The risk is coherence: three looks have to read as one system. D2 has to prove that with the light section and the dark chapter on the same board.

Recommendation and what the critique changed

Take the hybrid into D2 and D3. If D2 cannot make the three looks feel like one system, fall back to A alone, which scored highest on first-screen comprehension.

  • Bias checkScored A, B and C independently first. The hybrid had to outscore the best single direction to be chosen.
  • Laser geometryAll laser frames are built from one model that follows the reference sheet: web horizontal, head between the flanges, pierce then lead-in, a localized cut front.
  • Recorded statesScience frames show only the four recorded states with their iteration labels; the 3×3 tile is captioned as a visual arrangement.
  • LabelsEvery render is captioned “Illustrative render”, and no frame shows a process value.
  • Not yet shownThe laser-to-decision transition, the AI/EWSHM chapter and navigation behaviour belong to D2 and D3.
